# 📚 Free courses and learning platforms

## Computer science and coding
- 🆓 [CS50](https://cs50.harvard.edu): Harvard's famous intro to computer science. Free; certificate optional.
- 🆓 [freeCodeCamp](https://www.freecodecamp.org): full web development, data and Python curricula with projects.
- 🆓 [The Odin Project](https://www.theodinproject.com): full-stack path from zero to job-ready.
- 🆓 [Kaggle Learn](https://www.kaggle.com/learn): short practical courses in Python, SQL, ML.
- 🆓 [fast.ai](https://course.fast.ai): practical deep learning.
- 🆓 [Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/training/): Azure, Power BI, .NET and more.

## University-level courses
- 🆓 [MIT OpenCourseWare](https://ocw.mit.edu): lecture notes, problem sets and videos from MIT.
- 🆓 [Open University OpenLearn](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/): free short courses.
- 🎁 [Coursera](https://www.coursera.org): audit courses for free, or apply for **financial aid** to get the certificate.
- 🎁 [edX](https://www.edx.org): audit for free; certificates cost money, with aid available.
- 🎁 [FutureLearn](https://www.futurelearn.com): limited free access.

## Career certificates
- 🎁 [Google Career Certificates](https://grow.google/certificates/): data analytics, UX, project management. Financial aid available on Coursera.
- 🆓 [Cisco Networking Academy](https://www.netacad.com): networking and cybersecurity courses.
- 🆓 [AWS Skill Builder](https://skillbuilder.aws): free digital training tier.

## General subjects
- 🆓 [Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org): maths, science, economics, test prep.
- 🆓 [Saylor Academy](https://www.saylor.org): free college-level courses.
- 🆓 [Class Central](https://www.classcentral.com): search engine for free online courses from all platforms.

## How to actually finish a course
- Pick **one** course at a time.
- Schedule fixed study blocks (even 30 minutes).
- Build a small project from what you learn and put it on GitHub.
- Write a short post about what you learned. Teaching cements knowledge.

# 🤖 AI tools for students

Free plans change often. Check each tool's current limits.

## AI assistants
- 🎁 [Claude](https://claude.ai): writing, explaining, coding, studying. Free plan available.
- 🎁 [ChatGPT](https://chat.openai.com), 🎁 [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com): alternatives worth comparing.
- 🎁 [Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai): search with cited sources.

## Study and research
- 🎁 [NotebookLM](https://notebooklm.google.com): upload your notes or papers and ask questions about them.
- 🎁 [Elicit](https://elicit.com) and [Consensus](https://consensus.app): find and summarize academic papers.
- 🆓 [Zotero](https://www.zotero.org): reference manager.
- 🆓 [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net): spaced-repetition flashcards.
- 🆓 [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md): linked notes.

## Writing and translation
- 🎁 [DeepL](https://www.deepl.com/translator): high-quality translation.
- 🎁 [Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com): grammar checking.
- 🎁 [Canva](https://www.canva.com/education/): design, free for verified students and teachers.

## Coding and building
- 🎁 [GitHub Copilot](https://github.com/features/copilot): free for verified students via the [Student Developer Pack](https://education.github.com/pack).
- 🆓 [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co): models, datasets and courses.
- 🎁 [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com): experiment with models.
- 🆓 [Google Colab](https://colab.research.google.com): free notebooks with GPU access (limits apply).

## Use AI honestly and effectively
- **Check your institution's rules.** Some courses ban AI; others require disclosure.
- Use AI to **understand**, not to skip the learning. Ask it to quiz you, explain step by step, or critique your draft.
- **Verify facts and citations.** AI can invent sources.
- Never paste private data (passport numbers, passwords) into any tool.
- For applications, let AI help you structure and polish, but the story must be **yours**.

## Prompt ideas
- "Explain [topic] like I'm a first-year student, then give me 5 practice questions."
- "Here is my motivation letter. Point out weak spots, but don't rewrite it."
- "Make a 4-week study plan for [exam] with 1 hour per day."

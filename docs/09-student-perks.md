# 🎁 Student discounts and perks

Verification usually needs a school email or student ID. Offers change; check each page.

## Developer and tech
- 🎁 [GitHub Student Developer Pack](https://education.github.com/pack): free tools, cloud credits and more.
- 🎁 [JetBrains Student License](https://www.jetbrains.com/community/education/): free IDEs for learning.
- 🎁 [Microsoft Azure for Students](https://azure.microsoft.com/free/students/): free credits without a credit card in many regions.
- 🎁 [Figma for Education](https://www.figma.com/education/): free professional plan for students.
- 🎁 [Notion for Education](https://www.notion.so/product/notion-for-education)
- 🎁 [Canva for Education](https://www.canva.com/education/)
- 🎁 [Autodesk Education](https://www.autodesk.com/education): free access to design and engineering software.

## Office and productivity
- 🎁 Microsoft 365 Education: often provided by your school.
- 🎁 Google Workspace for Education: often provided by your school.
- 🆓 [LibreOffice](https://www.libreoffice.org): free alternative to Microsoft Office.

## General student discounts
- 🎁 [UNiDAYS](https://www.myunidays.com), [Student Beans](https://www.studentbeans.com): discounts on tech, food, clothing and more.
- 🎁 [ISIC card](https://www.isic.org): international student ID with discounts worldwide (fee applies).
- Transport, museums and cinemas often have student prices. **Always ask.**

## Free libraries and books
- 🆓 [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org), [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org), [OpenStax](https://openstax.org): free books and textbooks.
- 🆓 [DOAJ](https://doaj.org) and [arXiv](https://arxiv.org): legal open-access journals and papers. Your **university library** is also free for you. Avoid pirated copies ([why](10-stay-safe.md)).

# 💰 Scholarships

Cost tags: 🆓 free · 🎁 free with conditions · 💲 paid

## Where to search
- 🆓 [Scholarship Portal](https://www.scholarshipportal.com): searchable by country, level and nationality.
- 🆓 [Study in Europe](https://www.study-in-europe.org) and [Studyportals](https://www.studyportals.com): programmes plus funding.
- 🆓 Your target university's **"Fees & funding"** page. Many scholarships are only listed there.
- 🆓 Your home country's ministry of education and the embassies of your target countries.

## How to win scholarships
1. **Start early.** Most big deadlines are **October–February** for the following autumn.
2. **Read eligibility twice.** Nationality, age, work experience and prior degrees are the usual filters.
3. **Tell a story.** Reviewers read hundreds of essays. Show a problem you care about, what you did about it and what you will do next.
4. **Get strong references.** Ask early and give referees your CV and the deadline.
5. **Apply to many.** Aim for 8–15 scholarships across different sponsors.
6. **Never pay to apply** for a scholarship. See [Stay safe](10-stay-safe.md).

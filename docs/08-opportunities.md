# 🚀 Internships, hackathons and fellowships

## Internship and job boards
- 🆓 [LinkedIn Jobs](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/), [Indeed](https://www.indeed.com), [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com)
- 🆓 [Handshake](https://joinhandshake.com): student-focused, strongest in the US.
- 🆓 [Wellfound](https://wellfound.com): startup jobs and internships.
- 🆓 [Internshala](https://internshala.com): internships in India.
- 🆓 [EURES](https://eures.europa.eu): jobs and traineeships in Europe.

## Open source programmes
- 🆓 [Google Summer of Code](https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com): paid open-source work with mentors.
- 🆓 [Outreachy](https://www.outreachy.org): paid remote internships for people underrepresented in tech.
- 🆓 [MLH Fellowship](https://fellowship.mlh.io): project-based fellowship.
- 🆓 [Good First Issues](https://goodfirstissue.dev): find beginner-friendly issues to contribute to.

## Hackathons and competitions
- 🆓 [Devpost](https://devpost.com), [MLH](https://mlh.io): hackathons online and in person.
- 🆓 [Kaggle competitions](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions): data science.
- 🆓 [Codeforces](https://codeforces.com), [LeetCode](https://leetcode.com): competitive programming practice.

## Fellowships and youth programmes
- 🆓 [Opportunity Desk](https://opportunitydesk.org), [Youth Opportunities](https://www.youthop.com): curated listings of fellowships, grants and competitions.
- 🆓 [Global Youth programmes at the UN](https://www.un.org/youthenvoy/): events and calls.
- Check **your university, local embassies and cultural centres** (British Council, Goethe-Institut, Alliance Française) for local calls.

## How to get opportunities
1. Build **proof of work**: projects, writing, volunteering.
2. Apply regularly; set a weekly target (for example 5 applications).
3. Contact people directly: a short, specific message beats a mass email.
4. Follow up once after a week.
5. Keep a tracker with deadlines and results.
6. Learn from rejections. Ask for feedback when possible.

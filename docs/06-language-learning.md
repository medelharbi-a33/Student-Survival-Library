# 🗣️ Language learning

## Apps and platforms
- 🎁 [Duolingo](https://www.duolingo.com): gamified daily practice.
- 🆓 [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net): build vocabulary with spaced repetition.
- 🎁 [Memrise](https://www.memrise.com), 🎁 [Busuu](https://www.busuu.com): structured lessons.
- 🎁 [LingQ](https://www.lingq.com): learn by reading and listening to real content.
- 🆓 [Forvo](https://forvo.com): hear native pronunciation of any word.
- 🆓 [Language Reactor](https://www.languagereactor.com): dual subtitles on YouTube and Netflix.

## Speaking partners
- 🎁 [Tandem](https://www.tandem.net) and [HelloTalk](https://www.hellotalk.com): free language exchange with native speakers.
- 💲 [italki](https://www.italki.com): affordable one-to-one tutors.

## Free resources by language
- **English:** [BBC Learning English](https://www.bbc.co.uk/learningenglish), [British Council LearnEnglish](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org)
- **German:** [Deutsche Welle: Learn German](https://learngerman.dw.com), [Goethe-Institut free exercises](https://www.goethe.de)
- **French:** [TV5Monde Apprendre](https://apprendre.tv5monde.com), [RFI Savoirs](https://savoirs.rfi.fr)
- **Spanish:** [Instituto Cervantes](https://www.cervantes.es), [SpanishDict](https://www.spanishdict.com)

## A simple weekly plan
- **Daily (15–30 min):** app lesson + 10 flashcards.
- **3× a week:** listen to a podcast or video in the language.
- **1× a week:** a speaking session with a partner or tutor.
- **1× a month:** a practice test to measure progress.

Consistency beats intensity.

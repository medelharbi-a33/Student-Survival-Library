# 🌍 Work, volunteer and intern abroad

## Official EU and international programmes
- 🆓 [Erasmus+ traineeships](https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu): paid placements for students and recent grads in participating countries.
- 🆓 [European Solidarity Corps](https://youth.europa.eu/solidarity_en): volunteering and projects across Europe.
- 🆓 [EURES](https://eures.europa.eu): the EU job mobility portal.
- 🆓 [UN Volunteers](https://www.unv.org): volunteering with UN agencies.
- 🎁 [AIESEC](https://aiesec.org): international internships and volunteering (fees may apply).

## Work-and-travel style options
- 🎁 [Workaway](https://www.workaway.info): volunteer a few hours a day for accommodation and food (membership fee).
- 🎁 [WWOOF](https://wwoof.net): work on organic farms (membership fee).
- **Working holiday visas:** many countries offer them to young people from certain countries. Check the embassy of the country you want.
- **Au pair:** live with a host family; use official agencies and read contracts carefully.
- **Teaching English:** a TEFL/CELTA qualification is often required.

## Skilled work visas (after studies)
- 🆓 [Make it in Germany](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com): includes the Opportunity Card.
- 🆓 [Canada Express Entry](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry.html)
- 🆓 [GOV.UK Graduate route](https://www.gov.uk/graduate-visa)
- Many countries give graduates extra months to **stay and look for work**. Check yours.

## Remote jobs and freelancing
- 🆓 [We Work Remotely](https://weworkremotely.com), [Remote OK](https://remoteok.com), [Himalayas](https://himalayas.app)
- Freelance platforms (Upwork, Fiverr) are competitive. Start with a small portfolio and a specific service.
- Check your **tax and visa status**; working remotely from a tourist visa is often not allowed.

## Before you go: checklist
- [ ] Contract or written agreement
- [ ] Visa that **allows the activity** (volunteering, work, study)
- [ ] Insurance (health, travel)
- [ ] Emergency contacts and a copy of key documents
- [ ] Verified the organisation (reviews, official registration)

Never pay large "placement fees" to unknown agents. See [Stay safe](10-stay-safe.md).

No paywalls. No gatekeeping. Just curated, verified links and plain-English guidance to help you **learn, grow, and build a better future**.

## ⚡ 10-minute quick start

1. **Claim the [GitHub Student Developer Pack](https://education.github.com/pack)**: free tools, cloud credits and more.
2. **Pick one free course** from [docs/02](docs/02-free-courses.md) and finish week 1.
3. **Build a one-page CV** with [Europass](https://europass.europa.eu) or [Reactive Resume](https://rxresu.me).
4. **Shortlist 3 scholarships** you qualify for and write their deadlines down.
5. **Practise a language for 15 minutes a day.**

Small daily steps beat big plans you never start.

## 🧭 How this library works

- Every resource has a one-line description and a **cost tag**: 🆓 free, 🎁 free with conditions (aid, trial, student plan), 💲 paid but worth knowing.
- Links go to **official or well-established sources** wherever possible.
- Rules (deadlines, visa requirements, fees) change. **Always confirm on the official website** before acting.

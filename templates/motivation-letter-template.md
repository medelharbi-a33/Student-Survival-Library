# Motivation letter template (1 page)

**Your name** · your email · your city
**Date**

**Recipient name / Admissions Committee**
Programme or organisation name, Country

**Subject: Application for [programme / position / scholarship]**

Dear [Name or Admissions Committee],

**Opening (2–3 sentences).**
State what you are applying for and the specific reason it excites you. Mention one concrete detail that shows you researched the programme (a course, a lab, a mission).

**Why me (1 paragraph).**
Describe 2–3 experiences that prove you are ready. For each: what you did, what you learned, and the result. Link them to what the programme needs.

**Why you (1 paragraph).**
Explain what you will gain from this programme and how it connects to your long-term goals. Be specific about skills, not just "to grow".

**What I will contribute (2–3 sentences).**
Show what you will bring to the class, lab, team or community.

**Closing.**
Thank them, state your availability and sign off politely.

Sincerely,
**Your name**
